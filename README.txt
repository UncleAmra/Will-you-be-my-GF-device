README


	This was almost my first ever microcontroller project, I made this device for my girlfriend's birthday in 2024
while asking her to be my girlfriend at the same time. Thought it was a clever idea to ask her lol.
I made this years ago but did not add on github. We are now still together and happy, planning to marry her.
Hardwares used:
	ESP32
	Adafruit display
	LiquidCrystal display
	Buttons x3
	
External hardwares:
	NE555 timer 
	LEDS
	Resistors 
	Capacitor

Functions:
	button_1 - for next
	button_2 - to say yes
	button_3 - not connected 

	This device first will wish happy birthday with cake frames, and then after pressing the button, 
it will ask to if you wanna be my girlfriend(you can't say no since button_3 is not connected). Then it 
will say yay and then my image will appear few times and then heart image loops forever.

	While the the externel IC powered by the ESP32 will generate pulses to the LEDS, contantly blinking.
